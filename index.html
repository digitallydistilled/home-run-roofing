What Louis knows how to spot

Dead & poorly built valleys

Valleys that trap water and debris instead of shedding it quietly rot decking and leak long before anyone sees a stain.

Pitch & layout mistakes

Bad calls by previous roofers on how pitches and valleys were set up and shingled can turn into high-dollar repairs years down the road.

Heat & UV wear

Cracking, curling and granule loss from relentless Florida sun, caught early enough to plan for instead of panic over.

Flashing & water intrusion

Where roofs meet walls, chimneys and penetrations is where water finds its way in. Louis traces leaks back to the real source.

Gutter problems

Clogged, sagging or poorly pitched gutters send water into fascia, soffits and foundations instead of away from the house.

Masonry & stucco issues

Cracked stucco, failing mortar and chimney or parapet trouble let moisture in, and they're often tied directly to the roof above.
